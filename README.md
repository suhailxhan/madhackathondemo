Choosing the best ideas to build first
Here are funny ideas that would work in your project, grouped by where they’d go.

The two friends and the dialogue
Excuse generator: each play, the loser picks a random excuse, like “My UPI is down”, “I only have a ₹2000 note” or “I left my wallet at home”. Both friends argue about it.
Empty pockets gag: the loser turns his pockets inside out and only dust and a cobweb fall out.
Fake phone call: one friend pretends to get an urgent call and walks away, and the other drags him back.
Best of three: the loser begs “Best of 3?”, and a button runs the machine again with a score of Person A 0 – Person B 1.
Different endings: the loser can dramatically faint backwards, cry cartoon tears, or bow to the winner.
The chai tapri
The chai-wala: a third character shakes his head at every step and holds up a board saying “Udhar band hai” (“No credit”). At the end he hands over a bill.
A cow wanders across the grass during the toss, and a stray dog sits next to the tapri.
Biscuit dunk: a biscuit falls into the tea at the end, and the kettle whistles.
The machine
Silly loading messages: “Consulting the stars...”, “Asking mom for permission...”, “Calibrating chai quantum...” between the steps.
Pointless extra steps: a toggle that adds useless steps, like a tiny servo waving at the coin or the bell ringing twice, with a counter reading “Step 24/21 (we added 3 just because)”.
Fake bureaucracy: the Telegram approval needs a stamp from the “Chai Committee”. A Request recount button gets the answer “Denied” with a big stamp.
The coin gets a face: googly eyes and a moustache, saying “Wheee!” while it flies. In Out of machine mode it winks and says “I am double-headed, don’t tell.”
Cricket commentary: a replay with captions like “And it’s HEADS! What a shot!”.
Sound and the website
Soft sound effects: a trumpet fanfare for the winner and a short sad trombone for the loser, made in the browser.
Funny receipt: a printable “Certificate of Chai Debt” with 21 line items, like “Servo labour: ₹0.50”, and a fake stamp.
Silly stats panel: “Time wasted: 58 seconds instead of 1” and “Electricity used: enough to boil 0.0003 chai”.
Easter egg: typing “chai” on the page fills the screen with floating tea cups.
